    FAQ

    Are these ideas guaranteed to work?

    No. They are hypotheses. The viability score and validation experiment exist so you can test cheaply with real customers before committing your budget.

    How accurate are the cost estimates?

    They are directional estimates based on typical local prices. Get real quotes from suppliers before spending; prices and exchange rates change.

    What happens to my inputs?

    Your profile is sent to the server only to generate ideas and is not stored there. Past results are saved in your own browser and can be cleared at any time.

    What is demo mode?

    When the server has no AI key configured, it returns template-based ideas so you can try the experience. The badge on the form tells you which mode you are in.
